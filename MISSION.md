# Mission

Let a non-technical person empty her iPhone without fear.

She plugs the phone into her Mac or Windows PC, watches her photos and videos
copy to a drive she owns, sees the app prove the copies are good in two places,
and only then deletes them from the phone herself. Afterwards she can browse
them by month in a window she likes the look of, and ask her AI assistant to
change that look.

## What it must always do

1. Never lose or alter a photo: nothing in the app deletes, moves or
   overwrites a file in the library or the second copy.
2. Never delete from the iPhone. She does that in the Photos app.
3. Never say "safe to delete" unless every file matched its checksum in the
   library and in a second copy on another disk.
4. Work the same on macOS and Windows, from the same drive.
5. No subscription, no account, no server that has to stay on.

## What it does not try to do

Automatic or wireless backup, remote access, search by what is in a photo,
editing, or managing the iPhone's own Photos library.
